# Sunshine Whitening

Sunshine Whitening is a bilingual Thai and English ecommerce template for a premium soap brand. Its first audience is shoppers in Thailand buying small-batch personal-care products in Thai baht. The local version demonstrates browsing, product variants, cart, checkout, customer accounts, and product administration without external services.

The primary customer action is choosing a product and completing a clearly marked demo order. The primary store-owner action is reviewing products and changing local prices or stock. Product efficacy, certifications, reviews, and business policies must remain factual; the seeded content is illustrative and should be replaced before a commercial launch.
